⚙️ LIBRARY & HELP

How to: 1) Paste a SoundCloud, YouTube or MP3 link — or pick files / drop a folder — then Add. 2) Click any track to play — it keeps going to the next one. 3) Make playlists above the library (Morning, Evening…) — new music lands in the open one. 4) Tap EDIT for reorder, rename, remove, or moving tracks between playlists.

Good sources: soundcloud.com (public tracks), your own .mp3 host, any file host with https. If a track won't load, it was probably taken down or blocks hotlinking — try another link. Spotify plays in its own embedded player with its own buttons. YouTube plays in a small on-page player and pauses when a phone screen locks — MP3, SoundCloud and local files keep playing.
